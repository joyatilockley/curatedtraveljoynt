About Me

My love of travel wasn't something I chose. It chose me. I was brought into this world and immediately handed to parents who believed the best thing they could give me was the world itself. So we went. Everywhere.

I know what it feels like when everything clicks on a trip. When the water hits different, the mental load disappears and you're just fully there. Present. That's the feeling I plan toward for every client.

CTJ started as a love letter to my parents and the travel memories they gave me. It is growing into something I never expected — a legacy for my son Travis, in honor of the grandparents he'll know through the places we take him.

When I'm not curating someone's dream trip, you'll find me in a pool, with my people, moming, wifing, wrangling two labs, or shamelessly watching reality TV.
